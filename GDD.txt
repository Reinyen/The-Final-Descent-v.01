The Final Descent - Complete Game Design Document
Version 1.0 - Production Ready

Executive Summary
The Final Descent is a browser-based roguelike RPG where players command a three-person crew descending through a dying space station as it spirals into a black hole. Unlike traditional roguelikes focused on combat mastery, success means preserving knowledge and life—transmitting a final archive before reality unmakes itself.
Drawing inspiration from FTL's crew management, Slay the Spire's deck-building mechanics, Into the Breach's transparent information design, and Inscryption's visual storytelling, this game creates tension through cascading system failures rather than difficulty spikes.
The Echo system transforms unselected crew into hostile encounters, while the Combo Ability system rewards strategic crew preservation. Every run becomes a narrative of sacrifice and coordination as reality collapses.

Core Game Loop
The Macro Loop (Run Structure)
1. Crew Selection Phase
The game begins with all 6 crew members alive and available. The selection process:

Initial Draw: System randomly selects 3 crew members
Reroll Options (Player chooses ONE):

Full Reroll (2 chances): Discard all 3, draw 3 new from all 6
Single Swap (1 chance): Keep 2 crew, replace 1 with random draw from remaining 3


The Echo Consequence: The 3 crew NOT selected become "Echoes"—dead versions that haunt the station

Living Crew: Your active party with full abilities
Echo Crew: Hostile encounters, reality distortions, corrupted memories



This creates narrative weight—you're not just choosing your party, you're choosing who dies.
2. The Neural Network Map
Each ring presents as a JavaScript-rendered neural network visualization:

Initial State: Entire network appears dim and inactive
Starting Node: Pulses with light, energy travels along synaptic paths
Path Revelation: Completing a node sends energy bursts to 2-3 connected nodes
Progressive Illumination: Each completed path brightens permanently
Hidden Connections: Some paths only reveal after specific conditions
The Exit Node: Remains dark until 60% of ring is complete, then begins pulsing

Visual Design:

Nodes glow with different colors based on type (combat=red, resource=green, etc.)
Energy flows along paths like synaptic firing
Completed nodes remain lit but dimmed
Current position marked by bright pulsing ring
Mouse-over shows node preview without spoiling exact contents

3. Descent Structure

5 Rings total, each a larger neural network than the last
Ring 1: 7 nodes, Ring 2: 9 nodes, Ring 3: 11 nodes, Ring 4: 13 nodes, Ring 5: Final arena
Players must complete at least 60% of nodes before exit unlocks
Some nodes become inaccessible once passed (one-way paths)

4. The Archive System

Collect Data Fragments (universal currency for scoring)
Archive has 10 slots—choose what to preserve:

Genome samples (2 slots each, 500 points)
Black box recordings (1 slot each, 300 points)
Crew memories (1 slot each, 200 points)


Can overwrite slots but lose previous content permanently


The Micro Loop (Combat Resolution)
Streamlined Turn-Based Combat
Initiative System

Simple speed values: Fast (3), Normal (2), Slow (1)
Turn order = Character Speed + Ability Speed + Modifiers
Always visible, no hidden information
Player acts before enemies on ties

Shared Stamina System

Party shares 8 stamina points
Base regeneration: 4 points per round start

Jonas in party: +1 extra regeneration
Emergency Power active: +2 extra
System Strain (Ring 4+): -1 regeneration


Abilities cost 0-3 stamina
Free actions always available (0 stamina):

Basic Attack (character-specific damage)
Guard (reduce incoming damage by 50%)
Use Tool (consume item)



The Four-Fate Hand

Draw 4 cards from combined crew abilities each round
Each crew member has 6 abilities total (18 possible draws)
Combo Abilities appear when both required crew are alive
Cards show: Stamina cost, speed, damage/effect preview
Unused cards discarded at round end

Enemy Transparency

All enemy intentions visible
Damage numbers shown before confirmation
No hidden modifiers or surprise mechanics


Combat Mathematics
Core Damage Formula
Final Damage = (Base Ability Damage × Character Modifier × Status Multipliers) - Target Defense

Status Multipliers:
- Marked: +50% damage taken
- Lensed: 2x effect (damage or healing)
- Guardian Stance: -50% damage taken
- Weakened: +3 flat damage taken

Minimum damage is always 1 (unless immune)
Critical System
Base Critical Chance = 5%
Critical Damage = 1.5x

Modifiers:
- Kai's abilities: +10% crit chance
- Focused status: +15% crit chance
- Enemy Exposed: +25% crit chance against

Status System
Core Conditions (Only 6 Total)
Resource Pressures:

Mutation (0-100 scale): At 100, crew member transforms
Stress (0-100 scale): At 100, crew member panics and skips turn
Injury: -2 max HP until treated

Combat Modifiers:
4. Lensed: Next action happens twice (good or bad)
5. Delayed: Action resolves next turn
6. Marked: Takes +50% damage from next hit
Each status has clear visual indicator and numerical counter. No complex stacking.

Combo Abilities System

Combo Abilities are powerful synchronized actions requiring 2 specific living crew members. Unlocked after completing Ring 1.
Requirements:

Both crew members alive
Combined stamina cost (3-6 total)
Both crew members' turns consumed
1-per-combat limitation
Visually distinct cards with dual-character art

Turn-by-Turn Breakdown:

Turns 1-3: Environmental hazards (increasing mutation, gravity wells)

Final Design Notes
The Final Descent creates meaning through mechanical storytelling. The Echo system ensures every crew selection has narrative weight—those not chosen become the obstacles you face. The Combo Ability system rewards keeping crew alive, creating protection priorities beyond simple survival.
The neural network visualization serves both as intuitive navigation and thematic reinforcement—watching synapses fire as the station's consciousness fails. Every system interconnects: stamina forces tactical choices, mutations provide risk/reward, combos encourage crew synergy, and the Archive gives permanent meaning to temporary runs.
This is a game about inevitable failure that remains worth playing—because the question isn't whether you'll survive, but what truth you'll preserve before the end.
